Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

1. Introduction

Thank you for visiting the professional portfolio of Stephene Obare. This privacy policy outlines how personal data is handled when you interact with this static website. We value your privacy and aim to be transparent about how data is processed.

2. Information Collection & Processing

This website is a static portfolio and **does not host any database, server, or background tracking mechanisms**. We do not collect or store personal information in any central repository.

When you fill out the contact form, the form logic simply compiles your inputs (Name, Email, and Message) and redirects your browser to a standard mailto: link. The message is dispatched directly from your device's default email program (such as Outlook, Mail, or Gmail) to Stephene Obare's inbox. No intermediate server stores this transmission.

3. Cookies

We do not place tracking, analytics, or advertising cookies on your machine. The site utilizes standard CSS layouts and embedded content fonts loaded securely from CDN servers, which do not track individual users.

4. Third-Party Redirections

Our contact section features direct links to external professional platforms, specifically LinkedIn and WhatsApp. Please note that once you click on these icons, you will be redirected to their respective ecosystems. We do not control and are not responsible for the privacy practices or data processing policies of these external platforms.

5. Data Protection Rights

Since we do not collect, store, or process any personal data on a database, we do not maintain a register of personal files. However, you have full control over the emails you send directly to Stephene. You can request the removal or editing of past communications by contacting Stephene directly via his primary professional channels.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to align with regulatory adjustments or updates in contact pathways. Any adjustments will be reflected immediately on this page with an updated "Last Updated" timestamp.
